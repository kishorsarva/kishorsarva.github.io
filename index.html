Our Unique Approach: Prajñā → Pramāṇa

From Core Problem to Verifiable Proof.

Phase 1: Prajñā (Insight)

Starting with a core problem—from a client or our internal research—we perform deep scientific analysis and IP landscaping to chart a clear path to a novel solution.

Phase 2: Pramāṇa (Proof)

We build the proof: a working prototype plus a foundational patent portfolio that secures the innovation.

IP Framework (India)

We ensure patents are novel, inventive, and industrially applicable under the Indian regime:

  • Patents Act, 1970 (as amended).
  • Patents Rules, 2003 (as amended).
  • Manual of Patent Office Practice and Procedure (CGPDTM).

Operational Steps: structured prior-art search; drafting (Provisional/Complete specification); filing; Request for Examination (RFE); response to First Examination Report (FER) and subsequent hearings; claims strategy and portfolio building aligned with the Act, Rules, and Manual.
