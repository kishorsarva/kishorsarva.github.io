Resolving micro-scale interfacial physics to establish the evidence layer for industrial hardware. Our methodology bridges fundamental transport theory with commercial application via High-Fidelity DNS and Experimental Discovery.

Current Research Thrusts

MPF-T01

Droplet-Particle Interactions

Resolving collision efficiency and capturing dynamics between atomized droplets and coal dust particulates ($10\text{--}50\,\mu\text{m}$). This fundamental analysis de-risks the design of wet-scrubbing systems for subsurface mining safety.

DNS: Scavenging Feed

Validation of interfacial momentum transfer during high-velocity collision.

Exp: Collision Analysis

Quantitative assessment of particle wetting and aggregate stability.

MPF-T02

Interfacial Singularities

Investigation of self-similar singularities and breakup transitions in liquid filaments. Resolving these non-linear modes provides the mathematical foundation for high-precision atomization in medical healthcare and additive manufacturing.

DNS: Breakup Singularity

Capture of terminal thinning in non-Newtonian fluid filaments.

DNS: Regime Mapping

Investigation of critical parameters for stable jetting regimes.

The Research-to-Innovation Pipeline

Mathematical Resolution

Resolving instabilities and transport at the micro-scale via Direct Numerical Simulation.

Empirical Proof

Validation through laboratory-scale shadowgraphy to calibrate physical constants for industrial use.

Industrial Portal

Transition into industrial decision support systems (e.g., GG-MAP) for global deployment.
